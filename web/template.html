NFL Parlay Desk

Probabilities, not guarantees. No model reaches 99% on football. Win chances here are estimates checked against 11 seasons of games, and a parlay only hits when every leg does: four 66% picks hit together about 19% of the time.

How accurate is it?

Each season is predicted from earlier seasons only, so the model never sees the games it is graded on. Brier score and log loss measure probability quality; lower is better.

When it says 70%, does it happen 70% of the time?

Games grouped by how confident the model was in its pick. If the model is honest, the bar (how often the pick won) lines up with the tick (what the model predicted).

What parlays would have paid

Each week, parlay the model's most confident picks from different games, settle at the book's moneyline prices, and stake one unit per parlay.

Parlays hit about as often as predicted. They still lost money, because the sportsbook's margin is built into every price.

What it uses

Elo ratings with margin of victory, rest days, divisional games, and the sportsbook's own line with its margin removed.

What it tested and dropped

Injury reports and a quarterback-out flag. They added no accuracy beyond the line, which already reflects them, so they show as flags only.

Limits

Lines are a snapshot, not live. Same-game parlays are not supported because their legs are correlated. Check current prices before any bet.
